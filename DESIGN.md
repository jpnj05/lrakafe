# Design System: LraKafe

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Warm Corner"**

LraKafe looks like a printed menu left on a wooden table: cream paper, espresso ink, one clay-colored pen mark. The system is editorial rather than app-like — oversized serif headlines with a single italic accent word, generous vertical silence, photography framed in arches, and hairline rules instead of boxes. Nothing is pure white, nothing is pure black, and no surface is flat gray.

Density stays low. Sections breathe at 70–120px of vertical padding, type scales with viewport-aware clamps, and colour is used sparingly so the espresso panels land like a held breath. Warmth comes from the paper base and the photography's amber grade, not from gradients or ornament — the only ambient effects are two soft radial glows behind the hero.

Motion is slow and deliberate: Lenis smooth scroll, GSAP reveals with 0.9–1.2s easing, masked word-by-word headings, parallax on imagery, one pinned three-step ritual. Motion never delays reading and is disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Cream paper base, espresso ink panels, clay accent used on roughly a tenth of any screen.
- Fraunces display serif paired with Manrope for everything functional.
- One italic clay word per headline; never two.
- Arched hero imagery, rounded editorial cards, hairline rules at low opacity.
- Flat surfaces with two long, soft shadows reserved for photography.
- A recurring asterisk mark used as a stamp, a bullet and a decorative field.

## Colors

A toasted, low-chroma palette: dark roasted browns against cream paper, with terracotta and a muted gold as the only saturation.

### Primary
- **Roasted Clay** (#c0764a): the italic accent word in every headline, the arrow in a link, the hover fill on primary buttons, the scroll-progress gradient start. It is a mark, not a surface.
- **Deep Clay** (#a3562e): small type that must stay legible on cream — eyebrows, prices, owner roles, form labels. Never used for large fills.

### Secondary
- **Toasted Gold** (#d7a555): dark-panel accent only — the italic word in dark sections, the ritual progress line, the footer brand mark.

### Neutral
- **Cream Paper** (#f7efe2): the page base for every route.
- **Soft Cream** (#fcf7ee): cards, form panels, quote cards, the sticky header once scrolled.
- **Sand** (#ecdec9): quote band, media placeholders, monogram discs.
- **Sand Deep** (#ddc8ab): the only slightly heavier neutral, used for placeholder media.
- **Espresso** (#2b1a12): full-bleed dark sections, primary button fill, body ink on light.
- **Bark** (#5b3c2a): secondary text, labels, captions, hairline colour at 18% alpha.
- **Near-Black Ink** (#1d110a): footer ground; used nowhere else.

### Named Rules
**The Cream Margin Rule.** Every dark panel is a band inside cream, never the page itself. The page always begins and ends in cream paper.

**The Clay Rarity Rule.** Clay and gold together stay under ~10% of any viewport. Their scarcity is what makes them read as brand.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Fraunces (with Times New Roman, Georgia, serif)
**Body Font:** Manrope (with system-ui fallbacks)

**Character:** A high-contrast old-style serif with a slightly wonky personality against a neutral geometric sans. The serif carries voice — headlines, numbers, pull quotes, prices; the sans carries instructions, labels and paragraphs, so the page never turns into a magazine pastiche.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (400, clamp(2.8rem, 10vw, 4.4rem), 0.95): home hero only, two lines, tight tracking. Italic clay on the final word.
- **Page display** (400, clamp(2.4rem, 5.8vw, 4.9rem), 1): route titles and the invitation band, revealed word by word from a clipped baseline.
- **Headline** (400, clamp(2.1rem, 4.2vw, 3.7rem), 1.02): section headings, max ~20 characters per line.
- **Title** (400, 1.1–1.4rem, 1.2): product names, card titles, spec values, owner names, belief headings.
- **Body** (400, 0.86–1.2rem, 1.55–1.65): running copy in Bark, capped near 48–52ch for comfort. Never full-bleed line lengths.
- **Label** (700, 0.72rem, 0.28em, uppercase): eyebrows, navigation, buttons, badges, tasting notes, table headers. Eyebrows sit one step up at 0.75rem.

The frontmatter carries every step the components actually use, including the fluid clamps each display role resolves to, so a literal size in CSS should match one of those tokens rather than introducing a new one.

### Named Rules
**The Italic Turn Rule.** Exactly one word per heading is italic and clay. If the heading has no natural turn, use none.

**The Caps-Sparingly Rule.** Uppercase exists only at label scale (≤0.75rem) with ≥0.14em tracking. Never set a sentence in caps.

## Layout

A single centred container capped at 1240px, with fluid side gutters from 18px to 48px. Sections stack full-bleed and alternate cream, soft cream and espresso; radius, not borders, separates the bands.

Content sits in two shapes: **asymmetric splits** (hero copy 1.05fr / image 0.95fr; story image 0.92fr / copy 1.08fr) and **equal grids** (three product cards, three value cells, three owner cards, two-column spec tables). Nothing is centred except the closing quote and the invitation band.

Section padding is clamp(4.5rem, 8vw, 7.5rem); internal stacks land on 0.6 / 1.2 / 2rem. Hero imagery is a 340–600px arch, pinned sticky product imagery sits at 110px from the top, and the pinned ritual section occupies one viewport for 1100px of scroll.

Breakpoints are Bootstrap-compatible: 1200, 992, 768, 576. Below 992 the hero, story, about and product splits collapse to one column, the nav becomes a dark offcanvas drawer, and the pinned ritual stops pinning.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is **flat by default and tonal first**: depth is communicated by switching the whole band between cream, soft cream, sand and espresso. Borders are hairline only — 1px at `rgba(91, 60, 42, 0.18)` — and never read as boxes.

Two long, soft, low-opacity shadows are allowed, both attached to photography rather than UI: the hero arch sits on `0 40px 70px -46px rgba(43, 26, 18, 0.65)`, and the rotating stamp on `0 16px 34px -16px rgba(43, 26, 18, 0.45)`. Cards lift 6px on hover in the owner grid; everything else responds with colour or a fill slide, never a shadow.

### Named Rules
**The No-Card-Shadow Rule.** Panels and cards are flat at rest and stay flat on hover. Shadows belong to photographs and the stamp only.

## Shapes

Form language is **soft and edible**: pills for anything actionable (999px), 22px for imagery, 26–28px for panels and the map card, and a single architectural arch (`300px 300px 26px 26px`) reserved for the hero photograph. The smaller steps are 12px for inline alerts, 16px for the map caption panel, 18px for badges, tags and map pins, and 24px for the mobile drawer. Inputs break the pattern deliberately — squared, transparent, underlined only.

The asterisk (`*`) is the recurring glyph: set in Fraunces, clay or gold, used as a brand mark after the wordmark, as a rotating stamp, and as an oversized watermark in the invitation band.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** full pill (999px), label in 0.72rem uppercase with 0.16em tracking, 1rem × 1.65rem padding.
- **Primary:** espresso fill, cream text. A clay panel slides up from below on hover over 0.45s; the label stays cream.
- **Hover / Focus:** fill becomes clay; focus-visible uses a 2px deep-clay outline at 4px offset.
- **Ghost:** transparent with a 45%-alpha bark border, espresso text; the same clay slide on hover.
- **Light:** cream fill, espresso text — used only inside espresso bands.

### Chips
- **Style:** transparent, 1px bark border at 30% alpha, bark uppercase label at 0.64rem, pill radius.
- **State:** unselected is outline-only; selected inverts to espresso fill with cream text. Used for category filters and the product size selector.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 22px for product media, 26–28px for panels and the map.
- **Background:** soft cream on cream bands, cream on soft-cream bands; media sits on sand placeholder.
- **Shadow Strategy:** none; see Elevation.
- **Border:** 1px hairline at 14–18% bark alpha where a panel needs an edge.
- **Internal Padding:** clamp(1.5rem, 3vw, 2.4rem).

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** no box — a transparent field on a single 1px bark underline at 32% alpha, 0.65rem vertical padding, 0.92rem body type.
- **Focus:** the underline shifts to clay; the Bootstrap focus ring and glow are removed.
- **Labels:** 0.6rem uppercase bark label above each field; placeholders at 45% bark.

### Navigation
- **Style:** fixed masthead, transparent over the hero, then cream-86% with a 14px backdrop blur and a hairline bottom edge once scrolled.
- **Typography:** 0.7rem uppercase Manrope at 0.18em tracking; active and hover states draw a clay underline left-to-right.
- **Mobile:** hamburger in a 44px hairline circle opens a full-height espresso offcanvas; links are Fraunces at 2–2.5rem with gold numerals.

### Signature Components
- **Rotating stamp:** a 118px cream disc at the hero image's lower-right, carrying circular uppercase tracking text that spins over 24s, with a clay asterisk at its centre.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep the page base on Cream Paper (#f7efe2) and reserve espresso for full-bleed bands and primary buttons.
- **Do** set headlines in Fraunces at weight 400 with −0.025em or tighter tracking, and italicise at most one clay word.
- **Do** separate sections with tonal change and 1px hairlines at `rgba(91, 60, 42, 0.18)` rather than cards with borders.
- **Do** cap body copy near 48–52ch and keep labels uppercase at ≤0.75rem with ≥0.14em tracking.
- **Do** use 999px pills for actions and 22–28px radii for surfaces, and keep the arch radius unique to the hero photograph.
- **Do** grade photography warm (amber, brown, cream) and let it carry the saturation the interface refuses.

### Don't:
- **Don't** introduce pure white (#fff) or pure black (#000) — the lightest surface is #fcf7ee and the darkest is #1d110a.
- **Don't** add blue, green, pink or neon accents; clay, gold and brown are the entire hue range.
- **Don't** apply shadows to buttons, chips, cards or inputs, and don't add glows or glassmorphism beyond the header's 14px blur.
- **Don't** use more than one italic accent word in a heading, or set a heading in caps.
- **Don't** fill large areas with clay or gold — they are marks, not backgrounds.
- **Don't** let a dark band run edge-to-edge of the page without cream above and below it.
